04
LinearSVM best classifies self-disclosure signals.
Label: self_disclosure_flag (13.7% positive rate). TF-IDF unigrams+bigrams → classifier. 5-fold CV, F1-macro scoring. n = 10,867 comments. NaiveBayes fails on class imbalance — near-zero recall despite high precision.
5-fold CV · F1-macro
Model Comparison
