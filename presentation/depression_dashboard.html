Validation · HDBSCAN Cluster Geometry
The "real" depression cluster sits closer to a fitness false-positive cluster than to ordinary fitness comments.
c2 — 63 mental-health comments with genuine self-disclosure (avg signal 0.537, disc 44.4%). c3 — 236 fitness comments triggering depression vocabulary via weight-loss narratives ("I lost 20 kg", "I struggled…"). False-positive cluster. c4 — 33 ordinary fitness comments (workout reviews, near-zero signal). Cosine similarity in SBERT 384-d space: c2↔c3 = +0.175 (closest pair) vs c2↔c4 = −0.027 (orthogonal). The fitness narrative template — first-person + struggle + recovery arc — is what brings c3 close to c2 in semantic space.

05
LinearSVM best classifies self-disclosure signals.
Label: self_disclosure_flag (6.9% positive rate). TF-IDF unigrams+bigrams → classifier. 5-fold stratified CV, F1-macro scoring. n = 10,867 comments. NaiveBayes fails on class imbalance — near-zero recall (1 of 746 positives) despite perfect precision.
5-fold CV · F1-macro
Model Comparison
LinearSVM wins at 0.829 ± 0.014. NaiveBayes collapses to 0.484.
NaiveBayes paradox: precision 1.00 but recall only 0.001 — it catches just 1 of 746 positives. High accuracy (93.1%) is entirely majority-class collapse under 6.9% imbalance.
